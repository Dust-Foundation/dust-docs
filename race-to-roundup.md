# Race to Roundup

Three weeks. Every round-up you make on Robinhood Chain scores points. $5,000 in rewards, split by share of points.

Runs from September 14 to October 5, 2026, ending at 1 PM Eastern (17:00 UTC). The leaderboard lives in the app.

**Update, October 4:** the race gets a final stretch. It now ends on October 5 at 1 PM Eastern instead of midnight UTC on October 4, and for trades made in that added window, from 00:00 UTC on October 5 until the end, the $10 minimum trade size does not apply. Nothing scored before the window changes, and every other rule, including the $10 daily cap and the holding rule, stays as it is.

**Update, September 23:** the race was planned as ten days ending September 23. It now runs through October 4. Points already scored carry over, the daily cap and the holding rule are unchanged, and the top multiplier tier now needs 14 active days instead of all of them.

## Who can take part

* A wallet that is set up on Dust on Robinhood Chain: a basket chosen and Capture Everywhere switched on. [How to use Dust](how-to-use.md) walks through it in a few minutes.
* The wallet holds at least 1,000,000 $DUST. We check once a day. A day where the balance is below that doesn't score, and the wallet has to still hold it at the final check to be paid.

That's it. No sign-up form, no KYC, no minimum deposit beyond the round-ups themselves.

## How you score

* 1,000 points for every $1 of round-up captured onchain. A $0.62 round-up is 620 points.
* Counted round-ups are capped at $10 per wallet per day, so 10,000 points a day before multipliers.
* The trade that triggers a round-up has to be worth at least $10. In the final stretch on October 5 this minimum is lifted.
* A trade reversed within 24 hours (buy then sell, or sell then buy) doesn't count.
* Round-ups under one cent don't count.
* Keep your Stock Tokens in the vault until the campaign ends. Withdrawing them before the final check forfeits the wallet's score.

## Showing up counts

An active day is any day with at least one scoring round-up. Your total gets multiplied by how many days you were active.

* Fewer than 7 active days: 1.0x
* 7 to 13 active days: 1.25x
* 14 or more active days: 1.5x

## Referrals

Share your referral link from the app. Anyone who joins through it becomes your referee.

* A referee counts once they've scored 5,000 points, which is $5 of round-ups.
* You earn 5% of each active referee's points as a bonus. Their own score is untouched.
* With 3 or more active referees, your referral bonus is multiplied by 1.2x.
* The referral bonus is capped at 30% of your own score. You can't win on other people's trading alone.

## Rewards

$5,000 in rewards, split in proportion to points. Your share is your points divided by everyone's points. Payouts go out after the final check on October 5 at 1 PM Eastern, once the scores have been verified.

## The fine print

* Scores come from what the chain records: the round-ups captured, the trades that triggered them, the balances at each check. There's nothing to submit.
* During the campaign the keeper buys your basket once your captured change reaches $5, so gas doesn't eat into what you're investing. Round-ups are still captured the moment your trade lands; only the buy is batched.
* Wallets that split trades to dodge the caps, wash trade, or run several wallets as one can be removed from the leaderboard.
* The contracts don't change for this. Everything here runs beside the [audited](security.md) v3 contracts.
* If a rule needs clarifying mid-campaign, the clarification is posted on X and Telegram and here, and it applies from the time it's posted.
