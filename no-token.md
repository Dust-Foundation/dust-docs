# There is no Dust token

Dust has no token, no points program convertible to a token, and no plans for either.

This is unusual enough in web3 that it deserves an explanation rather than a shrug. Three reasons.

First, the product doesn't need one. Round-ups are denominated in USDG, the basket is tokenized stocks, gas is ETH. A native token would have no job except to be sold, and tokens without jobs become liabilities with tickers.

Second, history. The federal case that helped end Coinseed, the most direct predecessor to this product, was not about its round-up feature. It was about the token it issued to fund itself: an unregistered offering that raised money from hundreds of investors and gave the SEC its opening. The round-up idea has died once already from a fundraising token stapled to its side. We're not doing that.

Third, incentives. Dust earns a small fee on round-ups actually invested. That means we make money only when the product is doing its one job. A token would give us a second, louder business (number go up) that competes with the first. Every hour spent managing a token's market is an hour not spent on the product, and users can always tell where the hours went.

If you ever see a token claiming to be Dust, it is fake, full stop. This page is the permanent record you can point to.
